Crave

Say what you are craving. Eat what fits.

Reads the calories you burned from your health app, works out what fits your body today, turns a craving into three foods that fit, and finds the meals at the restaurant you are in that fit your diet.

Ruled

A record of each decision, the reason and the date.

Say what you decided and why. Ruled files it, brings it back on the review date, scores how it turned out, and shows you which kinds of decisions go well.

Keep

Never miss a birthday or a special occasion again.

Remembers the birthdays, anniversaries and occasions of the people who matter, reminds you early enough to do something about it, and drafts the message so it names a real thing.
