Privacy

Last updated October 14th, 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing you did not send us. The intake form asks for your name, your email, your business name if you give it, and your answers about the project. When you send it, those answers are stored in our project tracker and emailed to the Studio so we can write your scope. There are no accounts and no advertising trackers.

Server logs and analytics

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address, your browser type and the time of your visit. The site also uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies. We do not combine either with anything else.

Payments

Deposits and Care Plan payments are taken on Stripe's own pages. Stripe handles your card details under its own privacy terms. We see your name, your email and the amount paid, never your card number.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and your address so we can reply. We do not sell it and we do not share it.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to hello@ellingtonenterprises.co.
